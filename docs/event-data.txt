1.THE SCIENTIST FILES ( MURDER MYSTERY INVESTIGATION ) :
Purpose : 
To encourage participants to develop critical thinking, analytical reasoning, observation, teamwork, and problem-solving skills through an engaging, story-driven murder mystery investigation. 
Facilities Required :
College premesies
Date & Time :
                                                Oct 6 – 2026
                                 10:00AM – 12:30PM | Event Session
Rules
1. Participants will receive three fictional murder-mystery case files and must investigate all three using the evidence provided.
2. A final investigation report containing evidence analysis, deductions, timeline, and conclusion must be submitted within the allotted time.
3. Judging will focus on evidence-based reasoning, scientific analysis, accuracy, completeness, and clarity of the final report.

Facilities Required

Date & Time :
                                   Oct 6 – 2026
                 10:00AM – 12:30PM | Event Session
Rules
1. Participants will receive “What If?” scenarios set in 2065 and must provide creative, logical, and detailed responses.
2. Answers must be developed within the allotted time, with reasoning clearly supporting the proposed solution or outcome.
